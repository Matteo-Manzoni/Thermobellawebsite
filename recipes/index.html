How do Thermomix recipes work?

Where do the recipes live?
On Cookidoo®, Vorwerk's own platform, which the machine reads from directly. You pick a recipe and the TM7 sets its own times, temperatures and speeds as you go, telling you what to add and when. Vorwerk publish over 100,000 of them.
Do I have to follow them exactly?
No, and most people stop after a few weeks. Guided cooking is scaffolding: it teaches you what the machine does, and then you start cooking your own things in it. Manual mode is there the whole time.
Can I use recipes that are not Cookidoo?
Yes. Anything you already cook can be adapted, and a lot of it gets easier rather than harder. That conversion is most of what I help people with after they buy.
What should I cook first?
Something with no heat, so you learn the blade before you learn anything else. The first-week plan starts there and adds one new function a day for seven days.
Which recipes are worth doubling?
Sauces and stews, every time. The machine takes the same effort for twice the quantity, and half of it goes in the freezer. The weeknight plan flags the ones that reheat well.
