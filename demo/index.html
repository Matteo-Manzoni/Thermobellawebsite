The short version

Who runs it?
Isabella Spreafico, an independent Thermomix® advisor based in Twickenham, working with Thermomix since 2016.
Where?
In person in your own kitchen across South West London, covering 27 towns from Twickenham and Richmond to Kingston and Chiswick. Online demos reach anywhere in the UK and Ireland.
What actually happens?
We cook a real meal together around what you already eat, you run the machine yourself rather than watching, and then you eat it. Allow around two hours for an in-home visit.
What does it cost?
The demo is free and there is no obligation to buy. Vorwerk set the price of the machine and permit no discounting, so a TM7 costs the same from every advisor in the country; the current figures are on the TM7 page.

Which demo formats are there?

Three formats. All of them are free, and none of them ends with a decision on the day.

Thermomix demo formats, duration, location and cost
Format Length Where Who can come Cost
In-home cooking session 90 minutes Your kitchen, across South West London You, and anyone you invite Free
Personal virtual demo 45 minutes Video call, UK and Ireland One household Free
Online masterclass 60 minutes Live online, UK and Ireland Open group, cook along or watch Free

In-home sessions include a tour of Cookidoo® and the recipe you cooked, written down. Online formats are recorded and sent afterwards. If you are not sure which suits you, say so when you book and I will suggest one.

Ready when you are

Tell me roughly where you are and what you like cooking. I will call within one working day to arrange it, and if you are not sure which format suits you, say so and I will suggest one.
