Thermomix® essentials

What is a Thermomix?

Thermomix unites more than 20 kitchen functions in one intuitive device. Chop, weigh, blend, sauté, steam, knead, and so much more—guided by smart sensors and step-by-step recipes.

Discover the TM7 Book a Demo

A brief history

Developed by Vorwerk in Germany, the first Thermomix launched over 50 years ago. Each generation builds on the last, combining culinary expertise with precision engineering.

The TM7 is the latest evolution, introducing a full-touch display, on-device guided cooking, and even quieter performance. The short answer to what it replaces is in the FAQ, and the TM6 against TM7 comparison covers what changed. With Cookidoo® integration you can browse over 100,000 recipes directly on the machine.

Thermomix advisors like me provide personal training and support, ensuring every household gets the most from their investment.

23+ core functions in one bowl

Use one appliance to perform dozens of daily tasks, freeing up worktop space and simplifying clean-up.

Prep

Chop, grate, slice, grind, and mill with precision blades and variable speeds.

Bake

Knead dough, ferment yogurt, temper chocolate, and whip meringue to perfection.

Cook

Sauté, slow-cook, steam, sous-vide, and confit—all with precise temperature control.

Blend

Create silky soups, smoothies, nut butters, and frozen desserts in seconds.

Benefits for busy households

Save time

Guided recipes remove guesswork and automate timing so you can multitask or relax while Thermomix cooks.

Eat healthier

Prep wholesome meals from scratch, reduce additives, and hit nutritional goals with ease.

Reduce costs

Cut back on takeaways and single-purpose gadgets. One appliance replaces blenders, slow cookers, mixers, and more.
