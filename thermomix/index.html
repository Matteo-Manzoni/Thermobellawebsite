What is a Thermomix?

A Thermomix is a single countertop machine that weighs, chops, blends, kneads, steams and cooks in one heated bowl, following a recipe step by step on its own screen. It is made by Vorwerk, sold only through advisors or direct, and the current model is the TM7.

How does guided cooking work?

The recipe runs the machine, not you
You choose a recipe on the screen and it sets its own times, temperatures and speeds as you go, telling you what to add and when. You are not converting a recipe or guessing a setting. This is the part that makes the machine useful to people who do not think of themselves as cooks.
Where the recipes come from
Cookidoo®, Vorwerk's own platform, which the machine reads from directly. Vorwerk publish over 100,000 recipes on it, written for the machine rather than adapted from print. A new TM7 comes with a free three-month subscription, after which it is optional. See our own meal plans for what a week of it actually looks like.
Does it still work without a subscription?
Yes. The machine cooks in manual mode and keeps any recipes you have already saved. Guided cooking is the reason most people buy one, but it is not a licence you can lose your machine to.

How is it different from a food processor or a multicooker?

Mainly that it heats, and that it follows the recipe itself.

Is a Thermomix worth it?

The honest version
It is expensive, and whether it is worth it depends almost entirely on whether you will use it daily. People who cook from scratch a few times a week tend to get their money's worth. People who want a gadget for the weekend usually do not. Nobody should buy one without using one first, which is what a demo is for.
What it costs
Vorwerk set the price and permit no discounting, so a TM7 costs exactly the same from every advisor in the country. The current figures, and the two authorised ways to buy, are on the how to buy page.
Who it tends not to suit
Anyone who mostly reheats, anyone with no worktop to give it, and anyone who wants to buy it because it looks impressive. It earns its place by being used, not by being owned.

Where can you buy a Thermomix in the UK?

Two authorised routes, and no others
Through a Thermomix® advisor, or online direct from Vorwerk. Vorwerk state these are the only official channels in the UK and Ireland, and warn about third-party resellers, who cannot pass on support or warranty. The whole process, delivery and warranty are on how to buy one.
What an advisor is
An independent person, self-employed, trained and contracted by Vorwerk to demonstrate and sell the machine and to support the people who buy one. Isabella Spreafico has been one since 2016, working across Twickenham and South West London. More about her.
How to try one before deciding
A demo, which is free, takes around two hours, and happens in your own kitchen around food you already eat. The three formats are here, or ask for one.
