Where demos take place

In-person demos are hosted in your own kitchen, across South West London and the neighbouring Surrey and Middlesex borders. Virtual demos are welcome from anywhere in the UK and Ireland.

Areas covered for in-person demos

  • Twickenham, St Margarets and Strawberry Hill
  • Richmond, Ham and Petersham
  • Teddington, Hampton, Hampton Hill and Hampton Wick
  • Whitton, Isleworth, Osterley and Hounslow
  • Feltham, Ashford and Sunbury-on-Thames
  • Kingston upon Thames, Surbiton and Thames Ditton
  • East and West Molesey
  • Barnes, Mortlake, East Sheen, Chiswick and Brentford

Not on the list? Ask anyway. If it is a reasonable drive from Twickenham it is usually possible, and a virtual demo is always an option.
