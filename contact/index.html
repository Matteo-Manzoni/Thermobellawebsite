Which areas do you cover?

In-person demos are hosted in your own kitchen, across South West London and the neighbouring Surrey and Middlesex borders. Virtual demos are welcome from anywhere in the UK and Ireland.

Areas covered for in-person demos

  • Twickenham, St Margarets and Strawberry Hill
  • Richmond, Ham and Petersham
  • Teddington, Hampton, Hampton Hill and Hampton Wick
  • Whitton, Isleworth, Osterley and Hounslow
  • Feltham, Ashford and Sunbury-on-Thames
  • Kingston upon Thames, Surbiton and Thames Ditton
  • East and West Molesey
  • Barnes, Mortlake, East Sheen, Chiswick and Brentford

Not on the list? Ask anyway. If it is a reasonable drive from Twickenham it is usually possible, and a virtual demo is always an option.

Questions about coming to you

How far will you travel?
Roughly seven to eight miles of Twickenham for an in-home demo, which is the 27 towns listed above. Beyond that it depends on the day and how many of you there are; a group is worth a longer drive than one person, and it is always worth asking.
Do you charge for travel?
No. The demo is free and so is getting there. There is no call-out fee, no deposit and nothing to pay on the day.
What if I am outside the area?
A virtual demo covers anywhere in the UK and Ireland and works better than people expect: you cook in your own kitchen with your own ingredients while I talk you through it, so you still end up eating the result. Both formats are described here.
Do I need to host a group?
No. Plenty of demos are one person at a kitchen table. If you do want to invite people it makes the evening better, but nobody is required to fill a room.
What do I need to have ready?
A worktop, a plug and roughly two hours. Isabella brings the machine and the ingredients. If there is something you have always wanted to make, say so when you book and that is what gets cooked.
