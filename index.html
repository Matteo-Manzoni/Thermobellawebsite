Who, where, what it costs

Who is behind this?
Isabella Spreafico, an independent Thermomix® advisor based in Twickenham, working with Thermomix since 2016 and awarded Vorwerk's Diamond Team Leader award for 70+ sales in a year.
Where do you cover?
In person across South West London: Twickenham, Richmond, Teddington, Hampton, Kingston, Chiswick and 21 more. Online demos reach anywhere in the UK and Ireland.
What is a demo?
You cook a real meal with the machine in your own kitchen, around what you already eat, and then you eat it. Around two hours. The formats and lengths are here.
What does it cost?
The demo is free and nobody is asked to decide on the day. Vorwerk set the price of the machine and permit no discounting, so a TM7 costs the same from every advisor in the country; the figures are on the TM7 page. More of these in the full FAQ.

How does a demo actually work?

1

You tell me what you like cooking

A sentence is enough. The menu is built around that rather than around a script.

2

We cook it in your kitchen

You run the machine. Bring whoever you like; most people make an evening of it.

3

You eat, and then you decide

Or you decide nothing at all. Plenty of people book, enjoy the meal and say no, which is a perfectly good outcome.
