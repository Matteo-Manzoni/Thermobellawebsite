What the advisor journey looks like

Launch with a structured onboarding plan and ongoing mentorship designed for busy schedules.

Start with confidence

You’ll receive your TM7 demonstration unit, digital training materials, and weekly team calls covering demos, sales conversations, and customer care.

  • Step-by-step onboarding roadmap

  • Ready-made demo agendas and recipe packs

Perks of joining Thermomixbella

01

Getting your own machine

Vorwerk run schemes that let advisors work towards a machine of their own. The terms are theirs and they change, so ask what is running now.

02

Supportive community

Access advisor-only groups, WhatsApp support, and quarterly meet-ups.

03

Conferences and trips

Vorwerk run conferences and occasional trips for advisors who qualify. Whether any are running, and on what terms, is up to them.

04

How advisors are paid

Commission on what you sell, at rates Vorwerk set. What you earn depends entirely on how much you sell, and nobody here can tell you what that will be.

  • Time for onboarding calls and ongoing training

What you receive

  • Clear goals with rewards and incentives
