Thermomix TM7: what it does, and what it costs

It weighs, chops, kneads, steams and cooks in one bowl, and shows you each step on a 10-inch screen. What it does, what it costs, and what is in the box.

Book a free demo How a demo works

Smarter screen, guided cooking, and culinary intuition

The TM7 pairs professional-grade heat control with Cookidoo® intelligence so you can prepare everything from midweek meals to dinner-party showstoppers without second guessing.

10" adaptive display with step-by-step prompts

The vibrant touchscreen follows every recipe stage, adjusting temperatures and times automatically. Switch to open cooking whenever you feel creative.

  • Guided recipes with on-screen visuals and tips
  • Favourites, weekly meal plans, and shopping lists synced instantly
  • On-device scaling for quick portion adjustments

Precision sensors keep you in control

Integrated scales weigh to the gram straight into the bowl, and the machine holds temperature in 1°C steps, so results stay consistent whether you are steaming fish, tempering chocolate or kneading dough.

Engineered for quiet confidence

From breakfast smoothies to long sous-vide cooking, the TM7 is built to run quietly and hold a steady temperature.

Silent motor

New acoustic insulation makes the TM7 noticeably quieter than the TM6, so a conversation carries on while it runs.

Reinforced build

Upgraded base stability and blade assembly deliver smoother blending and longer-lasting performance.

Always connected

Dual-band Wi-Fi keeps Cookidoo®, software updates, and advisor content synced without requiring additional accessories.

Sensor-rich accessories

High-accuracy integrated scales weigh straight into the bowl. The Thermomix® Sensor, a probe for oven and hob cooking, is available separately.

Accessories ready for every cooking adventure

From the upgraded Varoma to new simmering baskets and scraper-friendly bowls, the TM7 kit makes multitasking effortless.

  • Enhanced Varoma with larger drip tray and stackable shelves
  • Thermomix® Sensor probe for oven and hob cooking, sold separately
  • Redesigned bowl with diamond-polished finish for easy cleaning

How much does a Thermomix TM7 cost in the UK?

Two authorised ways to buy it, and the price is the same from every advisor in the country.

Through a Thermomix® advisor

£1,349 UK

€1,549 in Ireland

Delivered, set up, and with someone to call afterwards.

Online, direct from Vorwerk

£1,374 UK

€1,579 in Ireland

The same machine, plus a £25 online processing fee.

Vorwerk permit no discounting, so no advisor can beat that figure and none can be beaten by it. What differs between advisors is who teaches you to use it and who answers the phone six months later. The rest of the common questions are in the FAQ.

Prices as published by Vorwerk UK, checked 1 September 2026. If the figure there has moved, that page wins.
