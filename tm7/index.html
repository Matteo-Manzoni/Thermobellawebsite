Thermomix® TM7

The smartest Thermomix yet for effortless everyday cooking

The TM7 unites a crystal-clear 10" display, precision sensors, and guided cooking intelligence to help you cook faster, eat fresher, and stay inspired. Explore what makes the newest Thermomix indispensable in modern kitchens.

Request a Demo Book a Demo

Smarter screen, guided cooking, and culinary intuition

The TM7 pairs professional-grade heat control with Cookidoo® intelligence so you can prepare everything from midweek meals to dinner-party showstoppers without second guessing.

10" adaptive display with step-by-step prompts

The vibrant touchscreen follows every recipe stage, adjusting temperatures and times automatically. Switch to open cooking whenever you feel creative.

  • Guided recipes with on-screen visuals and tips
  • Favourites, weekly meal plans, and shopping lists synced instantly
  • On-device scaling for quick portion adjustments

Precision sensors keep you in control

Weight, heat, and moisture sensors continuously monitor your cook, giving you chef-level consistency whether you are steaming fish, tempering chocolate, or kneading dough.

Engineered for quiet confidence

From breakfast smoothies to 20-hour sous-vide, the TM7 is built to run whisper-soft and flawlessly stable.

Silent motor

New acoustic insulation reduces peak noise levels by up to 30% compared to the TM6, keeping conversations flowing while you cook.

Reinforced build

Upgraded base stability and blade assembly deliver smoother blending and longer-lasting performance.

Always connected

Dual-band Wi-Fi keeps Cookidoo®, software updates, and advisor content synced without requiring additional accessories.

Sensor-rich accessories

High-accuracy integrated scales weigh straight into the bowl. The Thermomix® Sensor, a probe for oven and hob cooking, is available separately.

Accessories ready for every cooking adventure

From the upgraded Varoma to new simmering baskets and scraper-friendly bowls, the TM7 kit makes multitasking effortless.

  • Enhanced Varoma with larger drip tray and stackable shelves
  • Integrated smart thermometer for precise sous-vide and sugar stages
  • Redesigned bowl with diamond-polished finish for easy cleaning

TM7 technical specifications

Everything you need to know at a glance.

Bowl capacity 2.2 L (stainless steel)
Varoma capacity 6.8 L (full Varoma set including tray)
Speed range 40 – 10,700 RPM
Heat range 37°C – 160°C with 1°C increments
Dimensions 33.6 cm high × 25.3 cm wide × 40.5 cm deep
Weight 8.6 kg
Display 10" multi-touch glass panel
Connectivity Dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE
Included accessories Varoma, simmering basket, spatula, measuring cup and lid cover, mixing bowl with blade and insulation cover

Bowl capacity, Varoma capacity, dimensions and weight are as published by Vorwerk, checked 31 August 2026. Remaining rows are from manufacturer material not on that page. See the Vorwerk UK & Ireland specification page.
