Why TM6 owners love the upgrade

Advisors and customers share the improvements that made the switch worthwhile.

Immersive screen

The TM7’s larger display makes guided recipes easier to follow and icons more accessible for the whole family.

Quieter multitasking

Blend or knead without pausing conversations thanks to improved acoustic dampening.

Smarter accessories

The redesigned Varoma holds 45% more than the TM6's for multi-level steaming, and the optional Thermomix® Sensor extends guided cooking to the oven and hob.

Is the upgrade right for you?

If you rely on Cookidoo® daily, crave a bigger display, or want the quietest blend possible, the TM7 is a noticeable leap forward. The optional Sensor probe, bought separately, extends guided cooking beyond the machine for more advanced cooking—perfect for sourdough bakers, confectioners, and anyone experimenting with sous-vide.

TM6 owners can keep their existing accessories; most are compatible with the TM7. I can help you trade in or repurpose older models to make the transition smoother.
