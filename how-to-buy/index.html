How to buy one

Three steps, and the last two are mine to do.

  1. Cook on it first, or skip straight to ordering

    A demo is free, takes around two hours in your kitchen, and nobody is asked to decide on the day. If you already know, say so and we skip it.

  2. Order through Isabella

    One call, or the form with "I'm ready to order". I place the order with Vorwerk. Same price as anywhere in the country, no online processing fee, and I stay your contact rather than a helpline.

  3. Delivered, set up, and cooking

    Vorwerk deliver free, usually within 3 to 5 working days, with a two-year warranty and spare parts and support for up to ten years. I come back for the setup and the first recipes, and I am the person you call months later.

What the £1,349 includes

  • The TM7 base unit with the 10-inch screen
  • The 2.2 L mixing bowl with its blade and insulation cover, the lid and lid cover
  • The Varoma steaming set, the simmering basket and the spatula
  • Three months of Cookidoo® and its 100,000+ guided recipes
  • A two-year warranty from Vorwerk

The Thermomix® Sensor probe is sold separately. Contents, warranty and delivery as published by Vorwerk UK, checked 2 September 2026. Specifications are on the TM7 page.
