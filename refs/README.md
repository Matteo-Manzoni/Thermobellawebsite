## Read across them

The register that fits: **warm neutral ground (cream, tan, soft white), real food and kitchen
photography, a visible named person, generous whitespace, one persistent booking CTA.** Not the
bold food-brand look in `04` — this is a considered £1,349 purchase made on trust in an
individual, so it should read closer to `02` and `03` than to a snack brand.

The current site is not far off structurally. It already has the hero, the feature strip and
the booking CTA. What it lacks is the restraint and the photography quality in these
references, plus a real human presence now that the invented testimonials are gone.
