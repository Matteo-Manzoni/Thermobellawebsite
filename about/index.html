My Thermomix story

Cooking has always been my love language. The Thermomix helped me balance career, family, and flavour—now I help others do the same.

From client dinners to family suppers

I hosted hospitality events across Europe before settling in London. When I discovered the Thermomix, it transformed how I cooked for my own family—quick midweek meals, healthy batch cooking, and indulgent showstoppers all from one device.

Friends and clients wanted to know how, so I trained as an advisor. A decade later Thermomixbella supports thousands of cooks with demos, classes, and ongoing community events.

Mission & values

Family first

Cooking should bring people together. I help you make nutritious meals without sacrificing time with loved ones.

Make it sustainable

Batch cooking, seasonal produce, and minimal food waste are at the heart of every Thermomixbella plan.

Long-term support

Every customer receives ongoing coaching, community invites, and new recipe inspiration every month.
