My Thermomix story

I am Isabella Spreafico, an independent Thermomix® advisor based in Twickenham. I have run in-home Thermomix demonstrations across South West London since 2016, and virtual demonstrations across the UK and Ireland. My in-home demos are free, so you can try cooking with a Thermomix in your own kitchen.

From client dinners to family suppers

I hosted hospitality events across Europe before settling in London. When I discovered the Thermomix, it transformed how I cooked for my own family: quick midweek meals, healthy batch cooking, and indulgent showstoppers all from one device.

Friends and clients wanted to know how, so I trained as an advisor. A decade later Thermomixbella supports thousands of cooks with demos, classes, and ongoing community events. If you are nearby, the towns covered in person are listed in full, and a demo is free.
